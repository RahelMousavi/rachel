
# Gathering Information from Video Search Engines

Video search engines and online video platforms can be useful sources of information during the Footprinting phase.

Security researchers can analyze publicly available video information to discover details about users, channels, organizations, and their published content.

## YouTube Metadata

Metadata is additional information associated with a video that describes its properties and content.

By analyzing YouTube metadata, researchers can collect information such as:

- **Published At:** The date and time when the video was published.
- **Channel ID:** The unique identifier of the YouTube channel.
- **Title:** The title of the video.
- **Description:** Additional information provided by the video publisher.
- **Thumbnails:** Images representing the video.
- **Tags:** Keywords associated with the video.

## Mattw.io

[Mattw.io](https://mattw.io/) provides online tools for extracting and viewing YouTube video metadata.

The YouTube Metadata tool displays structured information about a video, including its title, description, publication date, channel information, and tags.

### How to Use

1. Visit the YouTube Metadata tool.
2. Enter the YouTube video URL or video ID.
3. Retrieve the available metadata.
4. Analyze the collected information for relevant details.

## Role in Footprinting

YouTube metadata can help security researchers:

- Identify information about video publishers and channels.
- Discover publication dates and video descriptions.
- Identify keywords and topics associated with an organization.
- Collect publicly available information about an organization's online activities.
