
# Searching for People on Social Networking Sites

Social networking websites are valuable sources of publicly available personal and organizational information.

People Search Websites help researchers discover information about individuals from different online sources.

## Information That Can Be Collected

These websites may provide access to publicly available information such as:

- **Email Addresses:** Publicly listed email addresses associated with an individual.
- **Images:** Publicly available photographs.
- **Social Media Profiles:** Links to profiles on different social networking platforms.
- **Blog Addresses:** Links to personal blogs and websites.
- **Other Public Information:** Additional information collected from publicly accessible sources.

## People Search Websites

The following websites are introduced in the course material:

### 1. Pipl

Pipl is a people search service historically used to discover information about individuals across online sources.

Website: https://pipl.com/

### 2. PeopleSmart

PeopleSmart is a people search service designed to find publicly available information about individuals.

Website: https://www.peoplesmart.com/

### 3. 411

411 is a directory and people search service that provides access to contact and directory information.

Website: https://www.411.com/

### 4. US Search

US Search is a people search service historically used to find information about individuals in the United States.

Website: https://www.ussearch.com/

### 5. Intelius

Intelius is a people search service that provides access to various types of public records and background information.

Website: https://www.intelius.com/

### 6. PeopleFinder

PeopleFinder is a people search service designed to help locate individuals and discover publicly available information.

Website: https://www.peoplefinder.com/

### 7. Veromi

Veromi is a people search service historically used to find information about individuals through public records and online sources.

Website: https://www.veromi.net/

## Role in Footprinting

People Search Websites can help security researchers:

- Discover publicly available contact information.
- Identify public social media profiles.
- Find connections between individuals and organizations.
- Collect information from different public sources.
- Understand an organization's publicly visible human network.

## Summary

Social networking platforms and People Search Websites are useful OSINT sources during Footprinting.

They can help researchers discover publicly available information such as email addresses, images, social media profiles, and personal websites.

**Important:** The availability and functionality of the listed services may have changed since the course material was created. Information gathering must respect privacy laws and authorization boundaries.
