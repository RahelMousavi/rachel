
# Footprinting Through Social Engineering

Social Engineering is the art of exploiting human behavior to obtain sensitive information.

Social engineers rely on the fact that people may not recognize the value of their information or may be careless about protecting it.

During Footprinting, social engineering techniques can be used to gather information about individuals and organizations.

## Social Engineering Techniques

### 1. Shoulder Surfing

Observing someone while they enter sensitive information, such as passwords or PINs, by looking over their shoulder.

### 2. Dumpster Diving

Searching discarded documents, storage devices, or other waste materials to find sensitive information.

### 3. Eavesdropping

Secretly listening to private conversations to obtain confidential information.

### 4. Identity Impersonation on Social Networking Sites

Creating fake identities or profiles on social networking platforms to deceive individuals and obtain information.

## Role in Footprinting

Social engineering can help attackers gather information that may not be available through technical reconnaissance alone.

It exploits human behavior rather than directly targeting technical vulnerabilities.

## Summary

Social Engineering is a technique for gathering sensitive information by exploiting human behavior.

Common techniques include Shoulder Surfing, Dumpster Diving, Eavesdropping, and Identity Impersonation on social networking sites.
