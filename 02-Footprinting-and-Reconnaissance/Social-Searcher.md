
# Social Searcher

[Social Searcher](https://www.social-searcher.com/) is a social media search and monitoring tool that allows users to find publicly available content across different social networking platforms.

It helps researchers discover mentions, profiles, and discussions related to specific names, keywords, or topics.

## Main Features

### 1. Social Media Search

Allows users to search for keywords, names, and topics across multiple social media platforms.

### 2. Multi-Platform Search

Social Searcher can collect publicly available results from different platforms, such as:

- Facebook
- LinkedIn
- Instagram
- YouTube
- Twitter (X)

### 3. Social Media Monitoring

Helps monitor mentions of specific keywords, brands, or topics across social networks.

### 4. Result Analysis

Allows users to review and analyze search results to understand how a person, organization, or topic is discussed online.

### 5. Export Results

Provides options to export collected search results for further analysis.

## Information Available

Depending on the platform and available public data, Social Searcher may help discover:

- Public social media profiles.
- Posts and comments containing specific keywords.
- Mentions of individuals or organizations.
- Public discussions related to a target.
- Links to relevant social media content.

## Role in Footprinting

Social Searcher can help security researchers:

- Discover publicly available information about individuals and organizations.
- Identify social media profiles associated with a target.
- Find public mentions of an organization or brand.
- Monitor discussions related to a target.
- Collect OSINT information from multiple social media platforms.

## How to Use

1. Visit the Social Searcher website.
2. Enter a name, keyword, or topic.
3. Select the available social media platforms.
4. Review the search results.
5. Analyze and export relevant information when available.
