
# WHOIS Footprinting

WHOIS is a query and response protocol used to retrieve registration and ownership information about internet resources, such as domain names and IP addresses.

WHOIS databases contain information about domain owners, contact details, DNS information, and IP address allocations.

## Information Available Through WHOIS

Depending on the domain and registration privacy settings, WHOIS lookups may provide:

- **Domain Owner:** Information about the domain registrant.
- **Contact Information:** Available administrative and technical contact details.
- **Registration Date:** The date when the domain was registered.
- **Expiration Date:** The domain's expiration date.
- **Name Servers:** DNS servers associated with the domain.
- **Registrar:** The company responsible for domain registration.
- **IP Information:** Registration and allocation details associated with IP addresses.

## Role in Footprinting

WHOIS Footprinting can help security researchers:

- Identify domain registration information.
- Discover available contact details.
- Identify domain registrars and name servers.
- Investigate IP address ownership and allocation.
- Collect information about an organization's internet presence.

## Summary

WHOIS Footprinting is a reconnaissance technique used to collect domain registration, contact, DNS, and IP allocation information.

It helps researchers identify information about domain ownership and the internet infrastructure associated with a target.
