
# Gathering Information Through Social Engineering

Attackers use social engineering techniques to collect information through social networking websites.

They may create fake profiles to deceive company employees and obtain sensitive information.

## Information Gathering Techniques

### 1. Creating Fake Profiles

Creating fake social media profiles to approach employees and attempt to obtain sensitive information.

### 2. Identifying Employee Interests

Analyzing employees' interests and using publicly available information to understand their preferences and activities.

### 3. Identifying Friends and Social Activities

Examining employees' friends, connections, and social activities to discover relationships and additional information.

### 4. Collecting Product-Related Information

Gathering information about products and technologies associated with employees' professional activities.

## What Users Do and What Attackers May Learn

### 1. Maintaining Profiles

Users maintain personal profiles, which may reveal:

- Contact information.
- Location information.
- Other publicly available personal details.

### 2. Connecting with Friends and Chatting

Social connections and conversations may reveal:

- Friends lists.
- Information about friends.
- Social relationships.

### 3. Sharing Photos and Videos

Shared media may reveal:

- Family members' identities.
- Personal interests.
- Background information.

### 4. Playing Games and Joining Groups

Online activities may reveal users' interests and preferences.

### 5. Creating Events

Public event information may reveal users' activities and participation.

## Organizational Information Gathering

Organizations also publish information that may reveal details about their business activities.

### 1. User Surveys

Surveys and feedback activities may reveal information about business strategies.

### 2. Promoting Products

Product promotions may provide information about an organization's products and services.

### 3. User Support

Customer support interactions may expose information about social engineering opportunities.

### 4. Recruitment

Job advertisements and recruitment activities may reveal information about an organization's platform and technology.

### 5. Background Checks

Employee recruitment and background-check processes may reveal the type of business and organizational requirements.

## Role in Footprinting

Social engineering-based information gathering can help attackers:

- Identify employees and their relationships.
- Discover personal interests and social activities.
- Collect publicly available contact and location information.
- Understand an organization's products and business activities.
- Identify technologies and platforms used by an organization.

## Summary

Social networking platforms can reveal information about individuals and organizations through publicly shared content and activities.

During Footprinting, attackers may analyze profiles, connections, interests, photos, events, recruitment information, and product-related content to understand their targets.

**Important:** Information gathering should respect privacy, consent, and authorization boundaries.
