
# AI-Powered OSINT Tools

AI-Powered OSINT tools use Artificial Intelligence to collect, process, and analyze publicly available information from different online sources.

These tools can help researchers automate information gathering, analyze large amounts of data, and identify relevant information more efficiently.

## Taranis AI

Taranis is an OSINT tool that uses Artificial Intelligence to extract information from various sources, including websites and news platforms.

### Main Applications

- **Cyber Risk Analysis:** Analyzes risks and threats in cyberspace.
- **Security News Monitoring:** Monitors security-related news about a country, organization, or individual.
- **Real-Time Threat Analysis:** Helps analyze emerging cybersecurity threats.
- **Media Risk Monitoring:** Monitors media content to identify potential risks.
- **Security Crisis Prediction:** Supports the analysis and prediction of potential security crises.
