
# Mobile-Based Social Engineering and AI Impersonation

## Mobile-Based Social Engineering

Mobile-Based Social Engineering uses mobile devices and applications to manipulate users into revealing sensitive information or performing actions that compromise security.

Attackers may exploit mobile applications and communication channels to reach their targets.

Examples of potential attack channels include:

- SMS messages.
- Mobile messaging applications.
- Malicious mobile applications.
- Deceptive links sent to mobile devices.

## AI-Based Impersonation

Artificial Intelligence can be used to create convincing fake identities and impersonate real individuals.

The slides introduce two main examples:

### 1. Deepfake Attacks

Deepfake attacks involve using technologies such as Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to generate fake videos or images of real people.

Attackers may use these manipulated media files to impersonate individuals and deceive others.

Deepfake technology can make fabricated content appear authentic, making it harder for victims to distinguish real media from manipulated media.

### 2. AI Voice Cloning

AI Voice Cloning uses Machine Learning algorithms to generate convincing copies of a person's voice.

These techniques can reproduce characteristics such as:

- Tone.
- Speaking style.
- Voice characteristics.
- Speech patterns.

Attackers may use cloned voices to impersonate trusted individuals and manipulate victims into taking specific actions.

## Summary

Mobile-Based Social Engineering uses mobile applications and communication channels to target users.

AI-based impersonation introduces additional risks through fabricated videos and cloned voices. Security awareness and independent verification of unusual requests are important defenses against these techniques.
