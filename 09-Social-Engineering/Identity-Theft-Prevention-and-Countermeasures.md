
# Identity Theft Prevention and Countermeasures

## Overview

Preventing identity theft requires protecting personal information, securing accounts, and being careful when responding to requests for sensitive data.

Individuals should take precautions both online and offline to reduce the risk of identity theft.

## Prevention Methods

### 1. Secure Personal Documents

- Keep documents containing private information in a secure location.
- Shred or securely destroy documents that are no longer needed.
- Avoid leaving sensitive documents where others can access them.

### 2. Protect Financial Information

- Review credit card statements regularly.
- Keep financial documents away from unauthorized individuals.
- Monitor online banking activities.
- Report suspicious transactions immediately.

### 3. Be Careful When Sharing Information

- Never disclose personal information to unverified callers.
- Verify requests for sensitive information before responding.
- Avoid sharing unnecessary personal details online.
- Do not publish identification numbers or payment card details on social media.

### 4. Secure Email and Communication

- Keep your email account secure.
- Be cautious of unexpected messages requesting personal information.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments.
- Delete sensitive information from messages when it is no longer needed, while following applicable retention requirements.

### 5. Protect Online Accounts

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Avoid reusing passwords across different services.
- Review account activity regularly.

### 6. Limit Publicly Available Information

Avoid exposing unnecessary personal information through social networking platforms.

Information such as phone numbers, addresses, identification details, and financial information should not be publicly accessible.

## What to Do If Your Identity Is Stolen

If you suspect identity theft:

1. Identify which accounts or personal information may have been compromised.
2. Change affected passwords and secure your accounts.
3. Contact the relevant bank or service provider.
4. Report unauthorized financial transactions.
5. Preserve evidence of suspicious activity.
6. Report the incident to the appropriate authorities.
7. Monitor your accounts for further suspicious activity.

## Summary

Identity theft can be prevented by securing personal documents, protecting financial information, verifying requests, limiting public information, and using strong account security.

Regular monitoring and quick action can help reduce the damage caused by identity theft.
