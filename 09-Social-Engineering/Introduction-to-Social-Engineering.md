
# Introduction to Social Engineering

## What Is Social Engineering?

Social Engineering is a non-technical method of obtaining information or gaining unauthorized access by exploiting human psychology.

It involves manipulating or persuading individuals into revealing confidential information or performing actions that may compromise security.

Unlike traditional cyberattacks that directly target technical vulnerabilities, social engineering attacks target people.

## The Human Factor

Human trust is one of the primary elements exploited in social engineering attacks.

Attackers take advantage of the fact that people may not recognize the value of the information they possess or may not take sufficient care to protect it.

An attacker may use psychological techniques rather than specialized hacking tools to obtain sensitive information.

## Kevin Mitnick

Kevin Mitnick was a well-known American computer hacker who later became a security consultant and author.

In his book *The Art of Deception*, published in 2002, he described how social engineering could be used to obtain passwords and confidential information.

His story illustrates how attackers can exploit human trust and behavior without necessarily relying on sophisticated technical tools.

## Summary

Social Engineering is a psychological approach to obtaining information or unauthorized access by manipulating people.

It demonstrates that even organizations with advanced technical security can remain vulnerable when employees are unaware of social engineering techniques.
