
# Insider Threat Indicators

## Overview

An insider threat may not be immediately noticeable. However, unusual activities involving accounts, sensitive information, or physical access can indicate a potential security incident.

Organizations should monitor suspicious behavior and investigate unusual activities rather than assuming that every abnormal event is malicious.

## Common Indicators

The following signs are identified in the course material.

### 1. Information Disclosure

Unauthorized disclosure or exposure of confidential organizational information.

### 2. Changes or Loss of Network Logs

Unexpected modification, deletion, or loss of network logs may indicate an attempt to hide suspicious activities.

### 3. Multiple Failed Login Attempts

A high number of unsuccessful attempts to access network resources may indicate suspicious account activity.

### 4. Unusual Access Times

Accessing organizational systems at unusual or unreasonable times may require investigation.

### 5. Modification or Loss of Sensitive Data

Unexpected changes, deletion, or loss of important organizational data.

### 6. Unauthorized Downloading or Copying

Downloading or copying sensitive information without proper authorization.

### 7. Unauthorized Physical Access

Accessing restricted physical areas or organizational assets without permission.

### 8. Unusual Business Activities

Activities that do not match normal business operations or an employee's responsibilities.

### 9. Accessing Multiple Accounts from Different Systems

Unusual access to multiple user accounts from different devices or systems may indicate compromised credentials or suspicious activity.

## Important Consideration

These indicators do not automatically prove that an insider attack is taking place.

For example, an employee may access a system outside normal working hours because of an approved task.

Organizations should investigate the context, compare activities with normal behavior, and use multiple indicators when assessing a potential threat.

## Summary

Insider threats can be identified through unusual access patterns, unauthorized data transfers, missing logs, suspicious physical access, and unexpected changes to sensitive information.

Monitoring these indicators helps organizations detect potential threats before significant damage occurs.
