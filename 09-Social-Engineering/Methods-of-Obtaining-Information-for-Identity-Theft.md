
# Methods of Obtaining Information for Identity Theft

## Overview

Attackers use different techniques to collect personal information that can later be used for identity theft.

These methods may involve physical theft, online activities, social engineering, or malicious software.

## Common Information-Gathering Methods

### 1. Physical Theft

Attackers may steal devices or personal belongings containing sensitive information, such as:

- Computers.
- Mobile phones.
- Wallets.
- Identity documents.

### 2. Internet Searching

Attackers search online sources and social networking platforms for publicly available personal information.

This information may help them impersonate victims or answer security questions.

### 3. Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate individuals into revealing confidential information.

For example, an attacker may pretend to be a bank employee or technical support representative and request personal details.

### 4. Dumpster Diving

Attackers search discarded documents and materials for sensitive information.

Examples include:
- Bank statements.
- Printed documents.
- Account information.
- Personal records.

### 5. Shoulder Surfing

An attacker observes someone entering sensitive information, such as a password, PIN, or payment card details.

This can happen in public places or crowded environments.

### 6. Phishing

Attackers send fraudulent emails or messages designed to trick victims into revealing sensitive information.

Fake websites may also be used to collect login credentials and personal details.

### 7. Skimming

Skimming involves capturing payment card information using unauthorized devices installed on or connected to legitimate card-reading equipment.

Attackers may use the stolen information to commit payment card fraud.

### 8. Unauthorized System Access

Attackers gain unauthorized access to a user's computer or account to obtain stored personal information.

### 9. Malware

Malicious software can be used to collect sensitive information from infected devices.

Depending on the malware, attackers may obtain:
- Login credentials.
- Personal files.
- Financial information.
- Other stored data.

## Collecting Information Through Fake Job Opportunities

Another method involves pretending to offer employment opportunities.

An attacker may create a fake recruitment process and request personal information from job applicants.

For example, a fake employer may collect identity documents, contact information, or other sensitive details through a fraudulent recruitment profile or website.

Professional networking platforms such as LinkedIn may be misused to make these opportunities appear legitimate.

## Summary

Identity thieves may collect information through physical theft, internet searches, social engineering, dumpster diving, shoulder surfing, phishing, skimming, unauthorized system access, and malware.

Organizations and individuals should protect personal information both online and offline.
