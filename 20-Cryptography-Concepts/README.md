
# Module 20 — Cryptography Concepts

## Overview

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of cryptography and its role in protecting information during storage and transmission.

It covers encryption algorithms, hashing, cryptographic hardware, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), digital certificates, digital signatures, secure communication protocols, disk encryption, and common cryptographic attacks.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you should be familiar with:

- The fundamentals and goals of cryptography.
- Symmetric and asymmetric encryption concepts.
- Substitution ciphers and message digest functions.
- Hardware-based encryption and cryptographic tools.
- Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) and digital certificates.
- Digital signatures and certificate validation.
- SSL, TLS, OpenSSL, and PGP.
- Disk encryption concepts and tools.
- Common cryptographic attacks and their countermeasures.
