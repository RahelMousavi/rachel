
# Wireless Antennas and Network Architecture

## Wireless Antenna Types

Wireless antennas are used to transmit and receive radio signals.

The module introduces three types of wireless antennas:

### 1. Omni-Directional Antenna

An Omni-Directional antenna transmits and receives signals in all directions.

### 2. Semi-Directional Antenna

A Semi-Directional antenna focuses wireless signals in a particular direction or area.

### 3. Highly Directional Antenna

A Highly Directional antenna focuses signals in a narrow direction.

## Wireless Network Modes

Wireless networks can operate in two main modes:

### 1. Ad-hoc Mode

In Ad-hoc mode:

- No Access Point (AP) is required.
- Wireless devices communicate directly with each other.
- It is commonly used when computers are located close to each other.

### 2. Infrastructure Mode

In Infrastructure mode:

- An Access Point (AP) is required.
- Wireless devices connect to the AP first.
- Devices communicate through the Access Point.
- Its coverage area is larger than that of Ad-hoc mode.

## Wireless Network Architecture

The module introduces three important terms related to wireless network architecture:

### Basic Service Set (BSS)

BSS is a component of wireless network architecture.

### Distribution System (DS)

DS is a component used in wireless network architecture to connect wireless network structures.

### Extended Service Set (ESS)

ESS is another component of wireless network architecture.

## Wireless Network Adapters

Wireless network adapters provide wireless connectivity.

According to the module, wireless network adapters may:

- Operate in both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz frequency bands.
- Provide high output power.
- Offer good wireless coverage.
- Be suitable for penetration testing.

## Wi-Fi Chalking

Wi-Fi Chalking refers to activities related to identifying and marking the availability of wireless networks.

The module introduces four related terms:

### 1. War Walking

Walking through an area with a Wi-Fi-enabled device to identify open wireless networks.

### 2. War Flying

Using a flying object to identify open wireless networks.

### 3. War Driving

Driving through an area with a Wi-Fi-enabled device to identify open wireless networks.

### 4. War Chalking

Drawing symbols in public places to announce the availability of open wireless networks.

## Summary

Wireless networks use different antenna types and can operate in Ad-hoc or Infrastructure mode.

BSS, DS, and ESS are important terms in wireless network architecture. Wireless adapters provide connectivity across supported frequency bands.

Wi-Fi Chalking activities include War Walking, War Flying, War Driving, and War Chalking.
