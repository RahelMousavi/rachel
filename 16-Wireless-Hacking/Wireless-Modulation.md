
# Wireless Modulation

## Introduction to Modulation

Wireless communication uses radio waves to transmit information through the air.

In wireless transmission, data bits are carried on a sine wave called a **carrier wave**.

The process of placing data bits onto the carrier wave for transmission is called **modulation**.

Modulation allows digital information to be transmitted over wireless communication channels.

## Types of Wireless Modulation

The following are three modulation techniques introduced in this module:

### 1. Frequency-Hopping Spread Spectrum (FHSS)

FHSS is a modulation technique in which the transmission frequency changes or hops among different frequencies.

### 2. Direct-Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS)

DSSS is another type of spread-spectrum modulation technique used in wireless communication.

### 3. Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing (OFDM)

OFDM is a modulation technique used in wireless communication.

## Summary

Modulation is the process of placing data onto a carrier wave for wireless transmission.

The three modulation techniques introduced in this module are:

- Frequency-Hopping Spread Spectrum (FHSS)
- Direct-Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS)
- Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing (OFDM)

Each represents a different approach to transmitting information over wireless communication channels.
