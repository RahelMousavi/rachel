
# OWASP Top 10 IoT Threats

## 1. Overview

The OWASP Top 10 IoT Threats identifies common security risks affecting Internet of Things devices and their ecosystems.

The course lists the following ten threats.

## 2. Weak, Guessable, or Hardcoded Passwords

IoT devices may use weak, easily guessable, or hardcoded passwords.

These credentials can make devices vulnerable to unauthorized access.

## 3. Insecure Network Services

Network services running on IoT devices may contain security weaknesses that attackers can exploit.

## 4. Insecure Ecosystem Interfaces

Interfaces associated with IoT ecosystems may introduce vulnerabilities.

These interfaces can include web, application, and other communication interfaces.

## 5. Lack of Secure Update Mechanisms

IoT devices may lack secure mechanisms for updating their firmware or software.

This can make it difficult to install security fixes and protect devices against known vulnerabilities.

## 6. Use of Insecure or Outdated Components

Using insecure or outdated hardware and software components can introduce vulnerabilities into IoT devices.

## 7. Insufficient Privacy Protection

Inadequate privacy controls may expose users' personal or sensitive information.

## 8. Insecure Data Transfer and Storage

Data transmitted between IoT devices or stored on them may not be adequately protected.

## 9. Lack of Device Management

Insufficient device management can make it difficult to monitor, configure, and maintain the security of IoT devices.

## 10. Insecure Default Settings

Default configurations may contain insecure settings that expose devices to potential attacks.

## 11. Lack of Physical Hardening

IoT devices may lack sufficient physical protection against unauthorized access, modification, or tampering.

## Summary

The ten IoT threats listed in the course are:

1. Weak, Guessable, or Hardcoded Passwords
2. Insecure Network Services
3. Insecure Ecosystem Interfaces
4. Lack of Secure Update Mechanisms
5. Use of Insecure or Outdated Components
6. Insufficient Privacy Protection
7. Insecure Data Transfer and Storage
8. Lack of Device Management
9. Insecure Default Settings
10. Lack of Physical Hardening

These threats highlight the importance of secure authentication, network services, software updates, data protection, device management, and physical security.
