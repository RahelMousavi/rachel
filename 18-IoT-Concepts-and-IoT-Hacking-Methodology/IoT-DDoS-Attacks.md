
# IoT DDoS Attacks

## 1. Overview

Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks are listed as one of the attack categories targeting IoT environments.

A DDoS attack involves multiple sources generating traffic or requests against a target, potentially disrupting the availability of its services.
