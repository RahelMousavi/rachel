
# Jamming Attack

## 1. Overview

A Jamming Attack targets wireless communications by disrupting the communication between network devices.

The purpose of this attack is to prevent legitimate devices from communicating normally.

## 2. How Jamming Works

In a jamming attack, a significant amount of malicious or interfering traffic is transmitted over a wireless communication channel.

This interference can prevent legitimate traffic from reaching its destination.

As a result, legitimate endpoints may become unable to communicate with each other.

## 3. Impact of Jamming Attacks

A successful jamming attack can cause:

- Disruption of wireless communications.
- Denial of service to legitimate users.
- Loss of connectivity between network endpoints.

## Summary

A jamming attack disrupts wireless communication by introducing interference into the communication channel.

This prevents legitimate traffic from being transmitted successfully and may result in a Denial-of-Service (DoS) condition.
