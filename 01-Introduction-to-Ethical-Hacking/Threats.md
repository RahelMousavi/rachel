# Threats

## 1. Overview

A **Threat** is any potential event, action, or circumstance that could cause damage to a computer system, network, or digital information.

Threats can be intentional or accidental and may originate from both external and internal sources.

## 2. Common Types of Threats

- Malware and ransomware
- Phishing and social engineering
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Insider threats
- Natural disasters
- Hardware and software failures

## 3. Importance of Threat Identification

Identifying potential threats helps organizations understand what could harm their systems and information.

Threat identification is an important part of cybersecurity risk management and helps security professionals select appropriate security measures.

## 4. Summary

A threat is a potential source of harm to a computer system or its data.

A threat may exploit an existing vulnerability and result in a security incident.
