# Cybercrime in Iran

## 1. Overview

Cybercrime refers to illegal activities involving computers, digital devices, networks, or the internet.

In Iran, cyber-related offenses are primarily addressed under the **Computer Crimes Act**, which was passed in 2009. The law defines several categories of computer-related offenses and establishes penalties for individuals who commit them.

The law aims to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of computer data and systems.

## 2. Main Categories of Cybercrime in Iran

### 2.1 Unauthorized Access
**Punishment:** 91 days to 1 year of imprisonment, or a fine of 66 to 264 million Rials, or both.

### 2.2 Unauthorized Interception
**Punishment:** 6 months to 2 years of imprisonment, or a fine of 82.5 to 500 million Rials, or both.

### 2.3 Computer Espionage
**Punishment:**
- Unauthorized access to classified data: 1 to 3 years of imprisonment, or a fine of 200 to 600 million Rials, or both.
- Providing classified data to unauthorized persons: 2 to 10 years of imprisonment.
- Disclosing classified data to foreign governments or organizations: 5 to 15 years of imprisonment.

### 2.4 Computer Forgery
**Punishment:** 1 to 5 years of imprisonment, or a fine of 165 to 825 million Rials, or both.

### 2.5 Data Destruction and Manipulation
**Punishment:** 6 months to 2 years of imprisonment, or a fine of 82.5 to 330 million Rials, or both.

### 2.6 System Disruption
**Punishment:** 6 months to 2 years of imprisonment, or a fine of 82.5 to 330 million Rials, or both.

### 2.7 Computer-Related Theft
**Punishment:** A fine of 20 to 165 million Rials if the original data remains available to its owner; otherwise, 91 days to 1 year of imprisonment, or a fine of 66 to 264 million Rials, or both.

### 2.8 Computer-Related Fraud
**Punishment:** 1 to 5 years of imprisonment, or a fine of 165 to 825 million Rials, or both, in addition to returning the obtained property.

### 2.9 Misuse and Distribution of Cybercrime Tools
**Punishment:** 91 days to 1 year of imprisonment, or a fine of 66 to 264 million Rials, or both.

## 3. Summary

Iranian law criminalizes various forms of cybercrime, including unauthorized access, data theft, computer fraud, espionage, and system disruption.

The severity of punishment depends on the type of offense, its consequences, and the circumstances in which it was committed.

Understanding these legal restrictions is essential for ethical hackers. All penetration testing and security assessments must be conducted with proper authorization and within clearly defined legal boundaries.
