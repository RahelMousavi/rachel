
# Role of AI and ML in Cybersecurity

## 1. Overview

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

**Machine Learning (ML)** is a subset of AI that allows systems to learn from data, identify patterns, and make predictions.

## 2. Applications in Cybersecurity

- **Threat Detection:** Identifying suspicious activities and potential cyberattacks.
- **Malware Detection:** Detecting malicious software based on its behavior.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Identifying unusual user and network activities.
- **Phishing Detection:** Recognizing suspicious emails, links, and attachments.
- **Automated Response:** Blocking malicious IPs and isolating infected systems.
- **Vulnerability Management:** Identifying and prioritizing security weaknesses.

## 3. Benefits

- Faster threat detection.
- Automation of security tasks.
- Real-time monitoring.
- Improved threat analysis.
- Faster incident response.

## 4. Challenges

- False positives and false negatives.
- Data privacy concerns.
- Adversarial attacks against AI models.
- Poor-quality training data.
- AI-powered cyberattacks, such as advanced phishing and deepfakes.

## 5. Summary

AI and ML improve cybersecurity by automating security tasks, detecting threats, and analyzing suspicious activities.

However, they do not replace human security professionals. Combining AI-powered tools with human expertise provides more effective security.
