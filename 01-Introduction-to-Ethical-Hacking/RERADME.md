# Module 01: Introduction to Ethical Hacking

---

## Overview

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of **Ethical Hacking** and **Cybersecurity**. It explores cybercrime, different categories of cyberattacks, hacker classifications, and the role of ethical hacking in protecting information systems.

It also provides an overview of three major EC-Council certifications: **CEH, CHFI, and CPENT**, highlighting their different objectives and areas of specialization.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

- Understand the concept of cybercrime and its impact.
- Differentiate between CEH, CHFI, and CPENT certifications.
- Identify and explain different types of cyberattacks.
- Understand the concept of hacktivism.
- Distinguish between White Hat, Black Hat, and Gray Hat hackers.
- Understand the fundamentals and responsibilities of ethical hacking.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Cybercrime

- Definition and common forms of cybercrime
- Impact of cybercrime

### 2. Cybersecurity Certifications

- CEH – Certified Ethical Hacker
- CHFI – Computer Hacking Forensic Investigator
- CPENT – Certified Penetration Testing Professional

### 3. Types of Attacks

- Passive Attacks
- Active Attacks
- Close-in Attacks
- Insider Attacks
- Distribution Attacks

### 4. Hacktivism and Hacker Classifications

- Hacktivism
- White Hat Hackers
- Black Hat Hackers
- Gray Hat Hackers

## Learning Approach

This module combines theoretical concepts with practical exercises in an isolated virtual lab environment.

All practical activities are restricted to owned virtual machines and authorized testing environments.
