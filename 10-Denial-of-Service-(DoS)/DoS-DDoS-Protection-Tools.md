
# DoS/DDoS Protection Tools

## Introduction

DoS/DDoS protection tools are security solutions designed to detect, filter, and mitigate malicious traffic.

They help organizations maintain the availability of their networks, applications, and online services during DoS/DDoS attacks.

## 2. Protection Approaches

DoS/DDoS protection solutions generally use different approaches depending on the type and scale of the attack.

Common approaches include:

- **Network-Based Protection:** Detecting and filtering malicious traffic at the network level.
- **Cloud-Based Protection:** Redirecting traffic to external infrastructure for filtering and mitigation.
- **Application-Based Protection:** Protecting web applications and services against malicious requests.
- **Hybrid Protection:** Combining on-premises security devices with cloud-based mitigation services.
