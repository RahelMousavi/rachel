
# Module 10 — Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS)

## Overview

This module focuses on Denial of Service (DoS) and Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, their techniques, the role of botnets, and the methods used to detect, prevent, and mitigate them.

DoS and DDoS attacks aim to disrupt the availability of systems, services, or network resources by overwhelming their resources or preventing legitimate users from accessing them.

## Learning Goals

By completing this module, you should be able to:

- Understand the concepts behind DoS and DDoS attacks.
- Recognize different categories and techniques of DoS/DDoS attacks.
- Understand how botnets contribute to large-scale DDoS attacks.
- Examine the impact of DDoS attacks through a case study.
- Identify tools used in DoS/DDoS attacks.
- Understand common countermeasures and protection solutions.


## Summary

This module explores how DoS and DDoS attacks affect the availability of systems and network services. It covers attack concepts and techniques, botnets, real-world attack scenarios, attack tools, and methods for preventing and mitigating these attacks.
